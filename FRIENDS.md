# Macro Diary: how to get it

It's a calorie and macro tracker that runs in your phone's browser. Gabe added your email to the allowed list.

## Log in

1. Open the link Gabe sent you.
2. Type your email. You'll get a 6-digit code by email (check spam). Type it in.
3. If you ever see that login page again later, just log in again. It's normal; logins expire after a while.

## Install it

**iPhone (Safari only, not Chrome):** after logging in, tap Share → **Add to Home Screen** → Add. Open it from the home screen. The home-screen app may ask you to log in **once more**, because iPhone keeps it separate from Safari.

**Android (Chrome):** after logging in, tap ⋮ → **Install app** (or "Add to Home screen").

Use the installed app, not a browser tab. On iPhone, Safari can erase a website's data if you don't visit it for 7 days; the home-screen app doesn't have that limit.

## Your data

- Everything you enter stays **only on your phone**. Gabe can't see it; there's no server or account. Gabe can only see which emails logged in and when.
- That also means nobody can recover it. Deleting the home-screen app, clearing Safari data or getting a new phone erases it.
- **Back up regularly:** Goals tab → Backup → **Export**, and save the file to Files / iCloud / Google Drive. To restore on a new phone, install the app, then Goals → Backup → **Import**.
- The backup file contains your weight, age and diary. Keep it somewhere private.
- When you scan a barcode, the number is looked up on Open Food Facts (a public food database), so they see the barcode and your IP address. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
- The app works offline, except barcode lookup.
